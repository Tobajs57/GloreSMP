01

Zero Tolerance Cheats: Hacked clients, X-ray modifications, and automated tools result in an immediate permanent ban.

02

Glitch Exploits: Duplication bugs and system exploits must be reported instantly; usage equals a permanent block.

03

No Real Money Trades: Selling items or accounts outside the approved official store is entirely prohibited.

Scamming Allowed: Trade scams are fair game. Always verify transactions carefully before confirming trades.

No Alt Farming Bots: Automated accounts designed to farm currency or resources unfairly will be stripped and banned.

Market Freedom: Auction flipping, cornering items, and bulk market manipulations are legal business tactics.

08

Respectful Chat: Hate speech, severe harassment, and doxxing are strictly banned with zero exceptions.
