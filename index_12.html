Rules

Keep it clean and fair for everyone.

  • 01No cheats: X-ray, hacked clients, and unauthorized modifications result in an immediate ban.
  • 02No exploiting bugs: if you find one, report it instead of abusing it.
  • 03No real-money trading: trading items or services for real currency outside the official store is prohibited.
  • 04Scamming: trade scams are fair game, so always double-check your transactions.
  • 05Raids & PvP: raiding bases and open-world PvP are standard parts of the survival experience.
  • 06No bot farming: automated alt accounts or bots for farming resources are strictly banned.
  • 07Free market: player-driven economy and flipping items is completely up to you.
  • 08Respectful chat: severe toxicity, hate speech, and harassment are not tolerated.
